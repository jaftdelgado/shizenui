## Context Integration

### Input & InputGroup

When wrapping `Input` or `InputGroup`, the `variant="secondary"` is applied automatically unless variant is explicitly set.

<ComponentExample src="svelte/surface/SurfaceInput.svelte">
  <SurfaceInput client:load />
</ComponentExample>

### Checkbox

When wrapping `Checkbox` or `CheckboxGroup`, the `variant="secondary"` is applied automatically
unless a variant is explicitly set on a standalone Checkbox or on the group.

<ComponentExample src="svelte/surface/SurfaceCheckbox.svelte">
  <SurfaceCheckbox client:load />
</ComponentExample>

### Radio Group

When wrapping `RadioGroup`, the `variant="secondary"` is passed to its Radio children
automatically unless a group variant is explicitly set.

<ComponentExample src="svelte/surface/SurfaceRadioGroup.svelte">
  <SurfaceRadioGroup client:load />
</ComponentExample>
