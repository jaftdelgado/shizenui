### Removable Tags

Removing a Tag does not remove it from `value`, so you must update the selection in your `onRemove` handler.

<ComponentExample src="svelte/tag-group/TagGroupRemovable.svelte">
  <TagGroupRemovable client:load />
</ComponentExample>

#### Removing the Selection

With `selectionMode="multiple"`, `Shift` with the arrow keys, `PageUp` or `PageDown`, `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`Home` or `End`, or `Shift`+click,
selects a range of tags, from the last tag you selected to the one you move to (or click). `Shift`+`Home` and `End` only move focus. Pressing `Delete` or `Backspace` on a selected tag removes every selected
tag: `onRemove` receives all their values at once. The remove button only removes its own tag.

<ComponentExample src="svelte/tag-group/TagGroupRemovableSelection.svelte">
  <TagGroupRemovableSelection client:load />
</ComponentExample>
